Weld Setter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Weld Setter is a reference and calculator app for welding settings. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app does not require an account, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not share any information with third parties. All calculations happen locally on your device.

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if features are added that require data collection), this page will be updated accordingly.
